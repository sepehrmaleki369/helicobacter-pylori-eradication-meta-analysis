# Risk-of-bias files

**Use `rob_clinician.csv`.** It is the only risk-of-bias file that feeds the
published analysis.

| File | Status | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| `rob_clinician.csv` | **Authoritative** | Cochrane RoB 2.0 for all 60 eligible trials, assessed by a clinically trained reviewer. Generated by `scripts/10_rob_clinician.py` from the reviewer's returned workbook. |
| `rob_assessments.csv` | Superseded, not used | Keyword-matching Python script (`rob_assess.py`). Assigned "Some concerns" to 62/62 trials; Cohen's kappa 0.00 against the clinical reviewer. |
| `rob_assessments_reviewer2.csv` | Superseded, not used | Large language model, trialled as a second reviewer. Assigned "Some concerns" to 60/62 trials; Cohen's kappa 0.07 against the clinical reviewer. |
| `rob_consensus.csv` | Superseded, not used | Conservative consensus derived from the two files above. |

## Why the earlier files were abandoned

Both automated approaches were effectively degenerate: each rated almost every
trial "Some concerns", so neither distinguished between trials and neither
agreed with the clinician better than chance. The clinical reviewer, by
contrast, returned 25 low risk, 31 some concerns, and 4 high risk, and
identified two included reports as non-randomised — an eligibility error that
neither automated method detected and that led to those trials being excluded
from the review.

The files are kept rather than deleted so the methodological history is
inspectable. They should not be cited or reused as risk-of-bias data.

## Declared harmonisation

For one trial (Ref 29), domains D2 and D4 were rated High on open-label design
alone. Eradication in that trial was ascertained by rapid urease test,
histology, and a 13C-urea breath test against a pre-specified 2.5% cut-off, so
lack of blinding does not support a High rating under the review's
pre-specified objective-outcome convention, which the reviewer applied to the
other 59 trials. Both domains were harmonised to Low, moving the trial from
High to Some concerns overall.

The reviewer's original entry is preserved in the `*_reviewer_original` columns
of `rob_clinician.csv`. The rule is a declared `HARMONISE` block in
`scripts/10_rob_clinician.py`, and the change is stated in the manuscript and
flagged in Supplementary Table S3.
